##  Overview

Smart Writer is a powerful AI extension for Standard Notes that transforms your note-taking experience. With **Lumo AI Writer** and **Nyra AI**, you can generate high-quality content, make structured decisions, and streamline your writing workflow all without leaving your notes.

### Key Features

-  **AI Writing Assistant**: Generate articles, emails, posts, code, and more directly in your notes.
-   **Nyra AI Decisions**: Get structured yes/no, choice, and score answers with confidence levels.
-  **10+ Text Tools**: Rewrite, summarize, expand, shorten, simplify, translate, and more.
-  **Multi-Device Support**: Works on Desktop, iOS, and Android.
-  **Private & Secure**: Your data stays within Standard Notes.

##  Installation

### Step 1: Install the Extension

1. Open **Standard Notes** (Desktop or Web).
2. Go to **Preferences** **General**  **Advanced Options**.
3. Scroll down to **"Install External Package"**.
4. Paste the following install URL:

```
https://stdnote.com/v3/install.json
```

5. Click **Install** and confirm if prompted.

### Step 2: Activate Your License

1. Purchase a license at [https://stdnote.com/licensing/buy.php](https://stdnote.com/licensing/buy.php).
2. Copy your license key (format: `LUMO-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXX`).
3. In Standard Notes, click the **Lumo AI icon** in the editor toolbar.
4. Enter your license key and select your blog name.
5. Now you also can create posts at stdnote.com/blog/your_blog_name from your standarnotes.
